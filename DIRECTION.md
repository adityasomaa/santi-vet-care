## Why the assignment stands (no factual grounds to re-roll)
Saput poleng encodes *rwa bhineda*, the balance of paired opposites — and the single most
important fact on this website is a binary: **Buka / Tutup**. The candi bentar is a gate
that is literally one form cleaved in two: a threshold that is either open to you or not.
The world is achromatic, which leaves exactly one accent to carry state and action — the
right economy for a reader who is panicking. It is unmistakably Denpasar and cannot be
confused with a US vet startup.

The ritual object is **not** printed as literal checkered cloth. What carries over is its
grammar: paired opposition, the modular square unit, an achromatic ground with one signal
colour, and the split-and-part threshold form.

## Challengers, fused and weighed (axes: audience identification, product clarity)
- *Luminescent alien understory* — reveals content only on pointer proximity. Hides
  information by default; disqualified by the panic-user requirement. Fuses poorly.
- *Orizuru crane, 32 folds* — Japanese craft grammar on a Balinese clinic; a 32-step
  metaphor over an intake form someone fills one-handed is actively wrong.
- *Creator-hardware bench* — the real contender. "One safety-orange key that is obviously
  the point" maps cleanly onto the WhatsApp button, and the amber one-value screen onto the
  open/closed plate. Loses on audience identification: gunmetal hardware is culturally
  mute here and reads cold for a care category.
- *Eighties toy catalogue* — its entire grammar is built on prices and stock codes, and
  this brief forbids prices outright. Fails on product truth.
